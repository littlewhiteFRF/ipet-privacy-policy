我们处理哪些信息

当你使用喵刻时，应用会处理你主动填写、拍摄、选择或导入的信息，包括：

  • 宠物档案，例如昵称、种类、生日、体重及备注；
  • 照护与健康记录，例如提醒、疫苗、用药、饮食、日记、照片和视频；
  • 你录入的兽医联系方式、账目和物资库存；
  • 若你使用 Apple 账号登录，Apple 可能向应用提供账号标识，以及你选择分享的姓名或邮箱。应用在设备上保存这些信息以维持登录状态。

应用使用这些信息来展示和管理宠物资料、安排本机提醒、生成你主动请求的报告或导出文件，并提供你启用的同步和共享功能。

信息保存在哪里

设备本地

未启用 iCloud 同步时，宠物档案和记录保存在你的设备上。应用没有用于收集这些记录的开发者自有服务器。

iCloud 同步

你可以在应用设置中选择启用 iCloud 同步。启用后，宠物资料会通过 Apple CloudKit 保存在与你的 Apple 账号关联的 iCloud 数据库中，以便在你的设备间同步。关闭同步并完全退出、重新打开应用后，会停止后续同步；这不等于删除已经保存在 iCloud 中的数据。

家庭共享

如果你创建或加入家庭共享，所选宠物档案、照护记录、提醒和库存会通过 iCloud 分享给受邀成员。日记、账目和个人设置不会加入家庭共享。受邀成员可以查看共享内容；请只邀请你信任的人。

备份、日历与提醒

你主动导出的备份、报告或分享文件由你选择保存或发送的位置管理。启用日历同步后，应用会把你选择的提醒写入 Apple 日历。应用提醒在设备上安排。

相机、照片与系统权限

当你拍摄或录制内容时，应用会使用相机；录制带声音的视频时会使用麦克风。当你添加已有图片或视频时，应用会使用 iOS 提供的照片选择界面。相关媒体用于你选择的宠物记录、备份或导出。

你可以在 iOS“设置”中管理相机、麦克风、照片、通知和日历权限。拒绝或撤销权限后，对应功能可能无法使用。

Apple 服务与第三方

  • iCloud / CloudKit：按你的选择提供设备间同步和家庭共享；数据由 Apple 按其服务条款和隐私政策处理。
  • Apple 登录与 App Store：Apple 处理身份验证和应用内购买。喵刻不会收到你的银行卡信息。
  • 家庭成员：只有你通过家庭共享邀请的成员可查看共享范围内的资料。

当前版本不使用第三方广告或分析 SDK，也不把宠物记录发送到开发者运营的服务器。我们不会出售你的个人信息。

保存期限与删除方式

保存在设备上的资料会留在设备中，直到你在应用中删除相关内容或移除应用。你可以通过邮件联系开发者，咨询资料删除或隐私请求。

iCloud 中的资料由 Apple 按你的 Apple 账号和 iCloud 设置保存。关闭同步不会自动清除已有的 iCloud 副本；请通过 Apple 提供的 iCloud 数据管理方式处理。家庭共享成员已同步或另行保存的副本，可能在停止共享后继续保留。你导出的备份和文件也需要在你选择的保存位置删除。

开发者不持有宠物记录的独立服务器副本，因此无法从开发者后台直接查看或删除你个人 iCloud 数据。若需要协助，请通过下方邮箱联系我们；Apple 云端数据仍需由你通过 Apple 账号管理。

Information We Handle

MomentCat handles information that you enter, capture, select, or import, which may include:

  • Pet profiles, such as names, species, birthdays, weight, and notes;
  • Care and health records, including reminders, vaccinations, medication, diet, diaries, photos, and videos;
  • Veterinary contact details, expense records, and inventory that you enter;
  • If you sign in with Apple, an account identifier and any name or email Apple provides with your permission. The app stores these details on your device to maintain your sign-in state.

We use this information to display and manage pet records, schedule local reminders, create reports or exports you request, and provide sync or sharing features you enable.

Where Information Is Stored

On your device

When iCloud Sync is off, pet profiles and records are stored on your device. The app does not use a developer-operated server to collect these records.

iCloud Sync

You may enable iCloud Sync in the app settings. When enabled, pet information is stored through Apple CloudKit in the iCloud database associated with your Apple Account, allowing sync across your devices. After you turn sync off and fully quit and reopen the app, future syncing stops; this does not by itself delete information already stored in iCloud.

Household Sharing

If you create or join a household, selected pet profiles, care records, reminders, and inventory are shared with invited members through iCloud. Diaries, expenses, and personal settings are not included. Invited members can view the shared information, so invite only people you trust.

Backups, Calendar, and Reminders

Exports and backups are managed by the location or recipients you choose. If you enable Calendar Sync, the app writes selected reminders to Apple Calendar. App reminders are scheduled on your device.

Camera, Photos, and System Permissions

The app uses the camera when you take photos or record video, and the microphone when you record video with sound. When you add existing media, the app uses the iOS photo picker. Media is used for the pet records, backups, or exports you choose.

You can manage camera, microphone, photo, notification, and calendar permissions in iOS Settings. Some related features may not work if you deny or revoke a permission.

Apple Services and Other Parties

  • iCloud / CloudKit: provides sync and household sharing when you choose to use them. Apple processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Apple Sign in and App Store: Apple processes authentication and in-app purchases. MomentCat does not receive your payment card details.
  • Household members: only people you invite can view information within the shared scope.

The current version does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send pet records to a developer-operated server. We do not sell your personal information.

Read Apple’s Privacy Policy

Retention and Deletion

Information stored on your device remains there until you delete the related content in the app or remove the app. You can contact the developer by email with questions or requests about deletion and privacy.

Information in iCloud is retained by Apple according to your Apple Account and iCloud settings. Turning sync off does not automatically clear existing iCloud copies; use Apple’s iCloud data management options. Copies already synced or saved by household members may remain after sharing stops. You must also delete exported backups and files from the locations where you saved them.

The developer does not keep a separate server copy of pet records and cannot directly view or delete data in your private iCloud account. Contact us by email if you need help; cloud data still needs to be managed through your Apple Account.
